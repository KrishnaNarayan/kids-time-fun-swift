## Description
Kids Time Fun makes learning to tell time genuinely fun. Designed for children in grades 1–3, it turns reading a clock into a friendly, rewarding game.

Kids practice at their own pace across six activities:
• Tell Time — read the clock and pick the right time
• Set Time — move the hands to match
• Time After / Time Before — reason about what comes next
• Elapsed Time — figure out how much time has passed
• Mixed — a bit of everything

As kids answer correctly they earn belts — from white all the way up — and an adaptive engine gently re-drills the specific skills they miss, building real proficiency instead of rote guessing. Multiple student profiles let siblings or a whole classroom each track their own progress.

Built for kids and parents:
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no third-party tracking
• No accounts and no data collection
• Clear, colorful, age-appropriate design
• Full VoiceOver accessibility support

Whether it's homework practice or classroom reinforcement, Kids Time Fun helps children build a skill they'll use for life.
